# Long-lived development policy

- One user-facing AI agent controls the CAD workflow. Engines and skills are tools, not independent authority.
- Source metadata (verified bibliographic record) is NOT content verification. Content verification is NOT expert validation.
- No Feng Shui rule becomes executable without edition+page citation, explicit school/convention, reviewer sign-off and positive/negative/edge tests.
- No QCVN/TCVN checking rule becomes active without scope, source clause, revision/effective dates, expert sign-off and reproducible fixtures.
- Legal/technical findings are advisory reports ONLY in this application; no unapproved CAD mutation. Do not disguise noncompliance or safety hazards.
- Keep design assumptions and unresolved questions visible. Do not fabricate measurements, directions, structural elements, or requirements.
- Deterministic geometry first. Never rely on LLM arithmetic where geometric code can calculate.
- Never commit copyrighted full books, commercial standard PDFs, customer drawings, personally identifying house/owner information or secrets.
- Every change to algorithm or source selection must include tests and a changelog entry; preserve past reports with version stamps.
